# Support and grievance policy

## RCS Travels support channels

The configured website support phone and WhatsApp number is +91 85860 88085. The configured support email is rcstravels.support@gmail.com. The Help page provides call, email, and WhatsApp actions. Use these channels for booking, payment, refund, document, account, privacy, and captain concerns.

## Information for a support request

Provide the booking reference or relevant account information, a concise explanation, date, and supporting payment reference or screenshots where appropriate. Send private evidence directly through the support channel. Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, or payment credentials. A public FAQ cannot inspect your account or establish a live refund status.

## Draft grievance review process

**Draft:** the legal pages identify Rajuddin as the grievance contact and describe review of booking, account, payment, complaint, and support records. Provide relevant evidence and the outcome you seek. A published formal ticket workflow or guaranteed review result is not established by this description.

## Pending support deadlines and appeals

**Pending:** acknowledgement deadlines, resolution deadlines, support operating hours, formal account-action appeals, and regulatory escalation details require confirmation. Do not promise instant replies or a fixed resolution time. Applicable statutory remedies are not removed by these operational references.

## Urgent safety support

For immediate danger, the service guidance is to contact 112 or the appropriate public emergency service first. Contact RCS Travels afterward when safe. Ordinary support and FAQ channels are not emergency dispatch.
