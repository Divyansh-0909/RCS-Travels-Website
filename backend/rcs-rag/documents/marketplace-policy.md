# Pending captain marketplace policy

## Pending marketplace feature status

**Pending product model, not verified live:** the confirmed product context describes off-app bookings posted by one captain and claimed by another. Current legal payment drafts explicitly do not establish these marketplace fees as live captain terms. Do not offer a marketplace transaction or describe the following economics as active platform ride charges.

## Pending marketplace posting and deposit

**Pending product model:** Captain A posts an off-app booking and sets fare and deposit. The deposit must be at least ₹50 and less than the fare. Captain B pays it to claim. Contact information is revealed only after a successful claim. This proposed deposit is separate from the live scheduled acceptance wallet hold.

## Pending marketplace completion settlement

**Pending product model:** settlement occurs after completion. Captain B receives fare minus deposit with no marketplace completion fee. Captain A's pre-fee amount is the deposit; the proposed platform fee is 10% of that deposit, leaving the rest for A. This is not the 10% commission calculation for ordinary platform rides.

## Pending marketplace cancellation settlement

**Pending product model:** if B cancels, the proposed platform fee is 12% of the deposit, B receives the rest, and A receives nothing. If A cancels, B receives the full held deposit with no fee charged to B, and A receives nothing. These amounts require implementation and approved publication before being used as live FAQ promises.
