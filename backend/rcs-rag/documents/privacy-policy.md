# Rider and captain privacy reference

## Draft privacy notice status

**Draft:** the existing privacy notices describe intended data handling and still require legal and product approval. This reference does not certify regulatory compliance, retention deadlines, or completion of every privacy control. Do not treat older dated legal-framework commentary as current legal advice.

## Rider information used by the service

The draft notice covers profile and contact details, date of birth and gender where provided, emergency contacts, saved places, pickup/drop coordinates, bookings, complaints, support records, payment/refund references, and necessary device or notification information. Data is used for account access, booking, routes, assignment, payments, safety, support, and fraud prevention. The draft says RCS Travels does not sell personal data.

## Captain information used by the service

The draft notice covers profile and contact information, uploaded licence and vehicle documents, live/recent location and timestamps, offers, trips, cancellations, complaints, wallet/payment records, and necessary app or notification information. Documents are used for eligibility review; trip information supports matching and service operation.

## Location permissions and trip visibility

Location supports pickup, routes, matching, trip status, and operational checks. Captains may need frequent or background location while available or serving a ride. Device permissions can be managed in operating-system settings, but required features may stop working. Exact native background behavior and final retention periods remain subject to verification; do not promise continuous accurate tracking.

## Service providers and data sharing

Relevant draft provider categories include authentication, maps/routes/places, Razorpay payments, WhatsApp/Meta messaging, Firebase notifications, and storage/database services. Captains and riders receive information needed for their booked trip. Do not disclose another user's private records through a FAQ.

## Trip-share link privacy

Treat a trip-share link as access to trip information for anyone who receives it. The draft notice describes rider first name, pickup/drop details, scheduled time, captain and vehicle details, and permitted live location exposure. Share it only with intended recipients. A private trip link must never be added to this policy corpus.

## Retention, correction, and deletion

The draft retention principle is to keep data as needed for operations, support, safety, disputes, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. It does not establish a verified automatic purge schedule. Riders have a supported account-deletion flow that rejects active rides, removes saved places, and anonymizes specified profile fields while preserving linked records. Do not promise deletion of every historical trip or payment record. Contact support for access, correction, deletion, or privacy concerns.

## Other people's data and security

Provide another person's details only when authorized and appropriate for the booking. Protect OTPs and payment credentials. Report suspected misuse promptly. Final minor-consent, privacy-request response times, provider retention, and formal legal notice terms remain pending approval.
