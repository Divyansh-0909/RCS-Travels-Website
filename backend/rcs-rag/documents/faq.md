# RCS Travels rider and captain FAQ

## Customer booking questions

### How do I book an RCS Travels ride?

Choose pickup, destination, vehicle, and available trip options on the website, or use the WhatsApp booking flow. Review the quote and confirm. For a scheduled ride, complete any required advance payment. A confirmed booking can still be waiting for driver assignment. See [Booking policy](booking-policy.md).

### How early can I schedule a ride?

The website booking endpoint accepts pickup more than 30 minutes and up to 7 days ahead. Ride Now supports immediate requests where available. Availability is not guaranteed. See [Scheduled rides](scheduled-rides.md).

### When will I get my captain's details?

Driver details appear after a captain accepts and is assigned. There is no guaranteed exact one-hour disclosure time. Check your booking status and tracking page. See [Scheduled assignment](scheduled-rides.md).

### Can I choose sharing or an outstation trip?

Sharing is available where offered and depends on route and capacity matching. An unmatched sharing booking can settle at its stored solo fare. Arrange outstation terms with support and confirm the actual quote. See [Shared and outstation rides](shared-and-outstation-rides.md).

## Customer payment and cancellation questions

### Do I need to pay before the ride?

Ride Now has no advance. Scheduled rides normally require a 10% Razorpay advance, with the remainder after completion. A zero required advance after applicable adjustments can confirm without checkout. Pay only the amount shown by your booking. See [Fares and payments](fares-and-payments.md).

### Can I pay in cash or UPI?

Use the booking's available final-payment options after completion. Supported flows include cash and Razorpay UPI; UPI depends on the captain's verified payout destination. Scheduled advance payment uses Razorpay. See [Payment methods](fares-and-payments.md).

### Will I be charged for cancelling?

Ride Now has no prepaid cancellation charge. A scheduled cancellation can retain 10% of the final customer payable fare, capped at ₹100 and the paid advance, when the assigned captain qualifies as near pickup or has reached. Valid listed reasons waive it; without qualifying proximity or paid advance, no charge applies. Confirm the live quote. See [Cancellation policy](cancellation-policy.md).

### Which rider cancellation reasons waive the fee?

Supported reasons include being asked to cancel, excessive delay, no movement toward pickup, wrong pickup, extra-money demands, wrong vehicle, threatening or abusive conduct, and an unavailable requested/confirmed carrier. Select the truthful listed reason. Other does not automatically waive the fee. See [Valid customer reasons](cancellation-policy.md).

### What happens when the captain cancels?

The booking returns to matching or scheduled offers. A scheduled advance stays with the booking while replacement is sought; captain cancellation does not automatically end the booking or issue an immediate refund. You can use supported cancellation or support. See [Cancellation and replacement](cancellation-policy.md).

### How much of my advance is refunded?

For an eligible captured advance, fee-free customer cancellation follows full refund. A chargeable cancellation refunds the advance less the calculated charge. No unpaid advance is refundable. See [Refund policy](refunds.md).

### When will my refund reach me?

Refund initiation, provider pending status, and processed confirmation are different stages. No approved universal bank-credit deadline is established here. Check the actual payment/refund status or contact support. See [Refund timing](refunds.md).

### Will a complaint give me an automatic compensation credit?

No automatic award is established from a complaint or AI recommendation. Existing authorized compensation can reduce a booking's post-coupon obligation. If you cancel, the reservation is released and does not pay the cancellation charge. Ask support about a disputed entitlement. See [Compensation policy](compensation-policy.md).

## Captain ride and earnings questions

### Which documents do I need as a captain?

The configured required list includes profile photo, driving licence, and vehicle-specific RC, insurance, road tax, fitness certificate, and All India permit. Conditional documents depend on the vehicle. Uploads require review; upload success is not approval. See [Captain verification](driver-verification-policy.md).

### Why do I need wallet money for a scheduled ride?

Scheduled acceptance reserves 10% of the booking fare from your wallet. It is separate from the customer's advance. Your hold remains until the booking completes or the customer cancels, including if you hand the ride back for replacement. It returns to you as its original owner. See [Scheduled acceptance deposits](scheduled-rides.md).

### What commission does RCS Travels charge?

The current base rule is 0% below ₹800 of payable driving fare after coupon and 10% at or above that threshold. Pass-through components are excluded and eligible rewards can waive commission. Use the booking settlement for your actual amount. See [Captain commission](driver-wallet-and-payouts.md).

### How do I earn commission-free rides?

The current service grants 3 commission-free rides for every 20 completed rides when eligible. A reward is consumed only when ordinary commission would be positive. Cancelled rides do not count. See [Captain rewards](coupons-and-rewards.md).

### Can I cancel after arriving at pickup?

The captain backend supports cancellation in assigned, en-route, and reached states with a valid supported reason. Rider no-show requires 30 minutes after recorded arrival. Once started, contact support. The incident is recorded for review. See [Captain policy](driver-policy.md).

### Does a complaint immediately fine or block me?

The current incident collectors do not authorize an automatic fine, strike, or block merely from a report or cancellation record. Incidents require review. Historical thresholds and pending launch rules are not guarantees of current automatic actions. See [Complaints and account actions](complaints-and-account-actions.md).

### When can my wallet balance be paid out?

The current admin batch flow requires at least ₹1 of positive available balance, a verified UPI payout destination, and no open payout. It rechecks eligibility before execution. No universal daily, instant, or on-demand payout promise is established here. See [Captain payouts](driver-wallet-and-payouts.md).

## Account, privacy, and support questions

### How can I delete my rider account?

Resolve active rides first, then use the supported account-deletion flow. It removes saved places and anonymizes specified profile fields; linked trip and financial records are not all erased. Contact support for additional privacy requests. See [Privacy reference](privacy-policy.md).

### Does the captain marketplace charge 12%?

The 12% marketplace cancellation fee belongs to a pending off-app captain marketplace product model, not a verified live ordinary-ride charge. Do not apply it to platform bookings. See [Pending marketplace policy](marketplace-policy.md).

### How do I contact RCS Travels support?

Use the Help page, call or WhatsApp +91 85860 88085, or email rcstravels.support@gmail.com. Include your booking reference and issue, never OTPs or payment credentials. No universal support deadline is established here. See [Support policy](support-and-grievances.md).

### What should I do in an emergency?

The service guidance is to call 112 or the appropriate public emergency service first, then contact RCS Travels when safe. FAQ and support channels are not emergency dispatch. See [Safety policy](safety-policy.md).
