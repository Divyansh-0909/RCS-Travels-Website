## Submit and record the issue

Give your account/booking/financial-entry reference, relevant dates, the
decision or error you dispute, your explanation and the outcome you seek.
Attach only relevant material. Do not send passwords, payment PINs or OTPs.
If a notice gives an in-app response or appeal deadline, use that route
promptly and retain its confirmation; do not assume a general support email
automatically pauses an in-app deadline.

The proposed internal service standard is acknowledgement within 48 hours
and an aim to redress within one month, subject to a shorter mandatory
deadline. This is a proposed RCS standard for captain complaints, not an
assertion that every captain complaint is a consumer complaint. It becomes
an operational commitment when adopted and must be supported by records.

## Response and decisions

RCS should identify a reference or record of the issue and seek only relevant
additional information. Affected captains should have a reasonable opportunity
to explain disputed evidence before a final adverse decision where law and
safety permit. An allegation alone must not be represented as proven conduct.

For credible urgent safety concerns, a provisional protective restriction
may be considered before a full response. Its purpose, reason and review
route should be explained to the extent permitted. The review must consider
both relevant evidence and the captain's response, and avoid duplicate
financial consequences for the same incident without a distinct lawful basis.

Communicate the material outcome, reasons where permitted, any account or
financial correction and the available next step. Keep notice, response,
decision and correction records so errors can be reviewed. A progress update
does not extend a legal time limit.

## Reconsideration and outside remedies

If you disagree, ask for reconsideration and identify an error or additional
evidence. Where practical, a person other than the original decision-maker
should review a material disputed outcome. RCS does not promise an independent
external adjudicator. An in-app appeal process, where available, should be
used according to its notified instructions as well.

You may approach a competent transport or labour authority, court, police or
other statutory forum where applicable to the issue and your legal position.
This process does not create a mandatory arbitration agreement, remove
non-excludable rights, require waiver of a lawful complaint, or permit
retaliation for a good-faith report.

Privacy requests use the same contact and the applicable Indian privacy rules.
A closed account does not prevent a legitimate complaint or recovery of an
undisputed amount due.
