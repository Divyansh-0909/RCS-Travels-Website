# RCS Travels — Refund and Cancellation Policy

Version: 1 November 2026. Completed drafting candidate; not adopted or in effect.

## Payments and the scheduled advance

Ride Now normally has no advance and no prepaid rider cancellation amount to
refund. A scheduled ride normally requires a 10% advance, calculated/rounded
by the booking service after applicable discounts or booking credits. A zero
required advance can confirm without checkout. The advance is part-payment
of the ride fare; it is not itself a cancellation fee.

## When rider cancellation is fee-free

No scheduled rider cancellation fee applies where no captain is assigned,
no qualifying near-pickup condition has been established, or one of the
disclosed fee-free reasons applies. These reasons include:

- The captain asks you to cancel.
- The captain is taking too long or is not moving towards pickup.
- The captain is at the wrong pickup location.
- The captain demands extra money.
- The vehicle differs from the booked vehicle.
- The captain is threatening, abusive or harassing.
- A requested luggage carrier is unavailable.

Select the truthful reason and contact support if the shown amount appears
wrong. Safety-related complaints can be investigated separately; a complaint
does not become proven misconduct merely because it was submitted.

## When a fee may apply

For a scheduled ride with an advance actually paid, the product may charge
when the assigned captain's qualifying fresh location is within 500 metres
of pickup, or a verified arrival has already established that condition,
and no fee-free reason applies. A stale/missing current location must not by
itself establish proximity. A verified arrival can remain relevant after the
current location becomes stale. GPS estimates can be wrong; you may dispute
the evidence and charge.

The charge is the lowest of 10% of the relevant customer-payable fare,
₹100, and the advance actually paid. Only that amount is retained; the
remaining paid advance follows the refund process. The current product
credits that cancellation amount to the assigned captain.

For example, a ₹2,000 payable fare with ₹200 advance can have a maximum
₹100 cancellation charge and ₹100 refund. A fee-free cancellation refunds
the ₹200 advance. The actual booking quote and any more favourable mandatory
rule determine your entitlement.

You must see the cancellation amount before confirmation. If relevant facts
change before submission and the amount changes, the service must request
confirmation of the revised amount rather than silently take a higher charge.

## Captain cancellation, replacement and missed pickup

A captain can cancel before the ride starts through supported states. The
booking can then return to matching for a replacement; a scheduled advance
remains attached to that booking while replacement is sought. Captain
cancellation is not an automatic notice that your booking has ended or that
a refund has already reached your bank.

If you do not want a replacement or the ride cannot be provided, use the
supported rider cancellation/support process. A driver-fault cancellation
must not be treated as rider fault. Any captured advance that is refundable
must be returned. Contact the grievance officer if a replacement is not
found, the pickup is missed or a fee is incorrectly shown. Refunds and any
compensation required by applicable law are separate entitlements; this policy
does not replace one with an optional voucher.

## Processing and payment errors

A refund due is initiated through the supported payment-provider process to
the original payment source where supported. Refund eligibility, initiation,
provider processing and bank credit are different stages. We will communicate
available status and follow up on a failure. No universal instant-credit or
fixed bank-credit deadline is promised by this policy. A mandatory deadline
continues to apply where the law sets one.

A cash payment cannot automatically produce a payment-gateway refund. Contact
support with the booking and receipt/transaction reference so the payment
and suitable return method can be verified. Never pay a processing charge,
scan a collection QR or disclose a UPI PIN to receive a refund.

Report duplicate payments, payment debited without booking confirmation or
an incorrect fare promptly. Do not assume a pending payment failed merely
because a notification is delayed. Support should reconcile verified debits,
refunds and provider records without collecting the same fare twice.
