## What to send

Give your booking reference or registered contact details, date/time, what
happened, the outcome you seek and relevant screenshots or payment references.
Keep sensitive information proportionate to the issue. Do not send passwords,
OTPs or payment PINs. If you cannot identify a booking, describe the issue
so we can tell you what information is reasonably needed.

## Acknowledgement and investigation

Our proposed service standard is to acknowledge a consumer complaint within
48 hours of receipt, provide a reference or identifiable written record,
and seek redress within one month. A shorter applicable legal deadline takes
priority. We will give you a copy or summary of the complaint as recorded
so you can identify a misunderstanding. These steps apply when this policy
is adopted; this candidate does not establish that they are already staffed.

We assess relevant booking, payment, location and communication records and
may request information from the people involved. A credible urgent safety
concern should be considered promptly for protective action. We aim to avoid
unnecessary disclosure of your information to the other party.

## Outcome and review

We will communicate the outcome and the basis for a material decision where
law permits, including an approved refund or correction and its available
status. If an issue cannot be resolved within the stated period, we will
explain the delay and next step; that explanation does not extend a mandatory
legal deadline. You may ask Rajuddin to reconsider a decision and submit
additional evidence. Where practical, someone other than the original
decision-maker should examine a disputed material decision. RCS is a small
proprietorship and does not promise an independent external adjudicator.

## External remedies

You may use the National Consumer Helpline at
https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/ or approach an appropriate consumer commission,
court, transport authority, police or other competent authority as applicable.
Internal review is not a prerequisite to a statutory remedy. This policy does
not claim that RCS is already a National Consumer Helpline convergence partner.

Privacy complaints use the same contact. We will apply the relevant Indian
privacy law and any statutory request or grievance deadline that is in force.
