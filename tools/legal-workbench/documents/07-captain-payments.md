# RCS Travels — Captain Payments Policy

Version: 1 November 2026. Completed drafting candidate; not adopted or in effect.

## Fare and payment records

Collect only the amount shown or lawfully authorised for the booking. The
rider uses supported payment/cash-recording flows; a pending screen or an
unverified screenshot is not proof of a completed payment. Do not ask for
an undisclosed surcharge or the same fare twice. Raise an error with the
booking reference and supporting payment information.

Scheduled customer bookings normally use a 10% advance and a remaining
payment after completion, with rounding, discounts and credits calculated
by the service. Customer advance, captain acceptance hold, commission,
cancellation compensation and payout are different entries; none should be
described as interchangeable deposits.

## Commission and pass-through charges

The current calculation charges 10% commission when the eligible driving
fare after the coupon is at least ₹800. Below that threshold it calculates
no commission. Specified toll, parking, airport-access and luggage-carrier
amounts are excluded from that calculation where recorded as pass-through
charges. A safer-route driving surcharge is part of the driving component.
Promotional commission relief may apply where the service explicitly records it.

The booking/statement should identify the fare, relevant adjustment and
deduction. This description records the inspected product calculation; any
mandatory fare-share or other applicable requirement still governs. It does
not approve an undisclosed deduction or settle tax treatment. Changes require
proper notice and must not retrospectively increase a completed ride's charge.

## Captain acceptance holds and balances

Where a scheduled acceptance requires a hold, review the displayed amount
before accepting. The inspected product uses a 10% scheduled acceptance hold
for a qualifying new acceptance, released when the booking reaches a supported
terminal completion/cancellation outcome. A captain cancellation can rematch
the booking without immediately releasing an existing hold. Request review
if a hold remains after the booking ends or matching fails.

A negative balance can restrict new work under the account rules. A cleared
payment must be reconciled against the actual debt; an excess collection
must not simply become unexplained revenue. Request the statement and dispute
an error before treating a balance as correct. An internal wallet/ledger is
not described here as a bank deposit, prepaid payment instrument or guaranteed
stored-value product.

## Cancellations and responsibility

The scheduled rider cancellation charge is capped at 10% of the relevant
customer-payable fare, ₹100 and the advance actually paid. Disclosed valid
reasons waive it. The product credits qualifying rider cancellation
compensation to the assigned captain; it is separate from commission.

A captain cancellation can return the booking for replacement. It does not
by itself prove fault or authorise an automatic arbitrary fine. A financial
responsibility decision must have a disclosed, lawful basis and an accessible
record/review route. Corrections, refunds and reversals should be reflected
once without duplicated recovery. Mandatory passenger remedies remain payable
where required, independently of an optional credit or another party's debt.

## Payouts, cash and account closure

Supported final UPI payments may be directed to the captain's verified payout
UPI account; other balances can require platform settlement. Cash received
directly is not a later UPI payout as well. Review which part of the fare was
actually collected, by whom and through which channel before seeking payout.

A payout must use verified payment details and available, reconciled money.
Initiated, processing, paid, failed and reversed are different states. This
policy does not promise an unverified daily/weekly payout cycle or provider
activation. RCS must disclose the applicable settlement arrangement before
captains rely on it; contact support if no clear arrangement has been provided.
An account closure does not forfeit undisputed earned money.

## Tax, errors and complaints

Each party must meet the tax obligations that law places on it. Applicable
taxes, statutory deductions and supporting statements must be explained;
this policy does not invent a GST rate, tax exemption or registration number.
A platform's statutory responsibility cannot be transferred by merely calling
the captain responsible for all tax.
