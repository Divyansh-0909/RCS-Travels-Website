# Decisions and evidence needed before adoption

Prepared: 1 November 2026. This is an internal working record, separate from
the candidate customer/captain wording. Drafting is complete; the questions
below are not answered merely by drafting it. No paid professional review is
being represented as a completed step or imposed as a substitute for the work.

1. **Territory and service model.** The owner confirmed pickups outside UP but
   did not identify every state/UT. Establish that list, the actual vehicle
   arrangements and applicable current operating conditions. Delhi/Haryana
   entries are research candidates, not established service locations or legal
   conclusions. Existing repository compliance findings remain open.
2. **Adult accounts and child travel.** The new candidate proposes 18+ account
   holders and adult-accompanied child passengers. Earlier legal copy says 15.
   Confirm this policy, implement age/booking acceptance and appropriate privacy
   controls, and reconcile all copies before changing the published rule.
3. **Complaints.** Proposed 48-hour acknowledgement/one-month redress targets
   need an incoming record, complaint copy, assigned handler, follow-up and
   correction process. Do not claim continuous staffing or NCH partnership.
4. **Money.** Verify the actual final-payment routing, payout-provider status,
   settlement arrangement, tax/invoice treatment and refund follow-up. No daily
   payout or fixed bank-credit time has been invented. Acceptance holds and
   replacement exhaustion require end-to-end verification.
5. **Cancellation and consequences.** Product arithmetic is not a determination
   of mandatory state-specific remedies. Confirm current missed-pickup/terminal
   handling, required compensation and the actual reviewed notice/appeal rules.
   Do not revive old raw-complaint thresholds from earlier drafts.
6. **Privacy.** Implement a category-specific retention/deletion schedule,
   verify actual vendors/storage geography and backup/provider deletion steps,
   and confirm native location behaviour. Account deletion preserves related
   records; the candidates explain that limit rather than promise full erasure.
7. **Source completion.** Finish primary-text retrieval and amendment checks
   where `data/sources.json` records incomplete/landing-page-only review. Read
   full final state rules and the 2026 consumer amendment Gazette before relying
   on a specific rule, threshold, exemption or remedy.
8. **Consistent publication.** After substantive decisions and prerequisites
   are resolved, record an adoption/version date and make the website constants,
   all three locale catalogs, captain surfaces, PDF and RAG copies consistent.
   Existing `DRAFT` indicators have not been disabled. A current task's request
   for final documents is not evidence that these controls already exist.

The workbench never turns an unresolved authority or implementation question
into an affirmative compliance statement. It can be used for further research,
drafting and verification immediately, without claiming legal certification.
