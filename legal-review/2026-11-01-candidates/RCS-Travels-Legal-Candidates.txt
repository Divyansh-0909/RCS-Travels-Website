Accounts and booking for another person

The account holder and person making the booking must be at least 18 and legally able to enter a contract.
Keep your contact details accurate and do not share your login or verification codes. An adult may book for
another adult with that person's knowledge and authority. Give them the booking details and privacy

Requests, confirmation and availability

Submitting a request does not guarantee a vehicle. A scheduled booking's payment confirmation is distinct
from a captain accepting the ride. Check the booking status, assigned vehicle, pickup and scheduled time.
Traffic, weather, road restrictions and vehicle availability can affect estimates. Tell support promptly if the
assigned vehicle or captain differs from the details shown. Do not enter a vehicle if you reasonably believe it
is unsafe.

not mean the rider's booking has ended. You may use the supported cancellation or support flow if you do
not want to wait. Refunds and any legal remedies remain available as explained in the Refund and

Fare and payment

Ride Now normally requires payment after completion. Scheduled rides normally require a 10% advance,
calculated and rounded by the service. Discounts or an available booking credit may reduce the advance,
including to zero. The advance forms part of the fare; it is not an extra booking charge. Pay only the
remaining amount shown after crediting an advance or adjustment.

Use the payment options displayed for your booking, including supported UPI or cash recording. Never give
a captain or support person your UPI PIN, OTP or bank password. Payment confirmation and receipts
should be checked against the booking. Contact support about a duplicate, missing or incorrect charge.

RCS Travels — Refund and Cancellation Policy


Version: 1 November 2026. Completed drafting candidate; not adopted or in effect.

Payments and the scheduled advance

Ride Now normally has no advance and no prepaid rider cancellation amount to refund. A scheduled ride
normally requires a 10% advance, calculated/rounded by the booking service after applicable discounts or
booking credits. A zero required advance can confirm without checkout. The advance is part-payment of the
ride fare; it is not itself a cancellation fee.

When rider cancellation is fee-free

No scheduled rider cancellation fee applies where no captain is assigned, no qualifying near-pickup
condition has been established, or one of the disclosed fee-free reasons applies. These reasons include:

     The captain asks you to cancel.

     The captain is taking too long or is not moving towards pickup.

     The captain is at the wrong pickup location.

     The captain demands extra money.

     The vehicle differs from the booked vehicle.

     The captain is threatening, abusive or harassing.

    A requested luggage carrier is unavailable.

Select the truthful reason and contact support if the shown amount appears wrong. Safety-related
complaints can be investigated separately; a complaint does not become proven misconduct merely because
it was submitted.

When a fee may apply

For a scheduled ride with an advance actually paid, the product may charge when the assigned captain's
qualifying fresh location is within 500 metres of pickup, or a verified arrival has already established that
condition, and no fee-free reason applies. A stale/missing current location must not by itself establish
proximity. A verified arrival can remain relevant after the current location becomes stale. GPS estimates can
be wrong; you may dispute the evidence and charge.

The charge is the lowest of 10% of the relevant customer-payable fare, ₹100, and the advance actually paid.
Only that amount is retained; the remaining paid advance follows the refund process. The current product
credits that cancellation amount to the assigned captain.

For example, a ₹2,000 payable fare with ₹200 advance can have a maximum ₹100 cancellation charge and
₹100 refund. A fee-free cancellation refunds the ₹200 advance. The actual booking quote and any more
favourable mandatory rule determine your entitlement.

You must see the cancellation amount before confirmation. If relevant facts change before submission and
the amount changes, the service must request confirmation of the revised amount rather than silently take a
higher charge.
