## Information and purposes

We handle the following categories as needed for the service you use:

| Information | Why it is used |
| --- | --- |
| Name, phone number, account identifiers and authentication results | Create and secure accounts, verify access and contact you about a booking. |
| Gender, date of birth and emergency-contact details where provided | Account information and relevant support or safety functions. These details must not be used for an undisclosed unrelated purpose. |
| Pickup/drop locations, device location when permitted, saved places and routes | Find pickup points, calculate routes/fares, match a captain and provide trip functions. |

We receive information from you, your device, people you authorise to book,
captains involved in your booking, service providers and support interactions.
Do not provide another person's information without authority. We do not sell
personal data. A booking request is not consent to unrelated advertising.

## Location and device permissions

You can enter a location or use supported map/device-location features.
Device settings control location and notification permissions. Refusing a
permission may limit the relevant feature; it does not authorise unrelated
collection. We do not claim the rider website continuously tracks you in the
background. Captains' location information is handled separately for dispatch
and active rides.

## Who receives information

An assigned captain receives relevant pickup, destination, contact and booking
details needed to serve the ride. Co-riders should receive only what a shared
trip requires. A trip-share link can disclose trip information to anyone who
receives or forwards it, including first name, pickup/drop details, schedule,
captain/vehicle details and available trip progress. Share links only with
people you trust.

We use providers for authentication, hosting/storage, mapping, payments,
messaging and notifications. Current integrations include Clerk for account
authentication, Google mapping services, Razorpay-supported payment/refund
flows, Firebase notifications and WhatsApp/Meta where you use that channel.
The provider receives information needed for its role. Its own notice may
also apply to processing it performs independently. Using UPI does not require
you to disclose your UPI PIN or OTP to RCS or the captain.

Relevant information may be shared with authorised personnel and professional
advisers for support, safety, accounting and claims, or in response to a valid
legal request. We assess a request's authority and scope where permitted.
A business transfer may require transfer of relevant records with appropriate
legal safeguards and notice. We do not promise that every provider stores all
information exclusively in India; applicable transfer and storage restrictions
must be observed.

## Retention and security

Different records serve different purposes. An account profile, precise
location, trip record, support file, payment reference and security log should
not automatically have the same retention period. We retain identifiable
information only for a legitimate service, safety, accounting or legal need,
and apply deletion or de-identification when that need ends, subject to a
documented legal hold. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup
or every provider when you close an account.

Access should be limited to authorised people and providers. Appropriate
technical and organisational measures must protect the information. No system
is completely secure; report suspected misuse promptly. Where a security
incident triggers a legal notification obligation, that obligation applies
independently of whether you have complained.

## Your choices and requests

You may ask to access or correct your information, withdraw a consent, request
account closure or deletion, and raise a privacy grievance. We provide the
rights required by applicable law and consider other reasonable requests.
Use the contact above; identify the account and what you want changed. We may
verify identity proportionately without asking for passwords, payment PINs or
unnecessary identity documents.

Withdrawal may prevent a feature that needs the information, but must not be
used to remove unrelated statutory rights. Some records must remain for
accounting, a pending dispute, security or a legal duty. We will explain a
material refusal or retention exception where law permits. Deleting the rider
account removes listed profile identifiers and saved places in the current
account flow; it does not itself erase every linked booking/payment record.

## Children, changes and applicable law

The proposed Rider Terms require an adult account holder and an accompanying
adult for a child passenger. Do not create an account for a child. Contact us
if a child has supplied personal information so we can assess appropriate
action and any consent/retention obligations.

This notice does not assume that every provision of the DPDP Act and Rules
has already commenced. We must follow Indian privacy requirements as they
apply and update notices, consent and request handling when a new obligation
takes effect. Publishing this notice does not, by itself, establish compliance.

Material changes will be communicated as required, with a new version/date.
New consent will be sought where required for a changed purpose. Your right
to complain to a competent authority remains available under applicable law.
