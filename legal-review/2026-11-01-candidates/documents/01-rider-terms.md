## Accounts and booking for another person

The account holder and person making the booking must be at least 18 and
legally able to enter a contract. Keep your contact details accurate and do
not share your login or verification codes. An adult may book for another
adult with that person's knowledge and authority. Give them the booking
details and privacy notice. Do not send us another person's documents or
sensitive information unless needed and authorised.

A child may travel only with a responsible accompanying adult under this
policy. An adult booking does not by itself make an unaccompanied child ride
available. Seats, restraints, luggage and vehicle capacity must suit the trip.
Contact support before booking if you need assistance that the booking form
does not describe.

## Requests, confirmation and availability

Submitting a request does not guarantee a vehicle. A scheduled booking's
payment confirmation is distinct from a captain accepting the ride. Check
the booking status, assigned vehicle, pickup and scheduled time. Traffic,
weather, road restrictions and vehicle availability can affect estimates.
Tell support promptly if the assigned vehicle or captain differs from the
details shown. Do not enter a vehicle if you reasonably believe it is unsafe.

If a captain cancels before the ride starts, the booking may return to matching
for a replacement. That does not mean the rider's booking has ended. You may
use the supported cancellation or support flow if you do not want to wait.
Refunds and any legal remedies remain available as explained in the Refund
and Cancellation Policy.

## Fare and payment

The booking must show the fare or calculation basis and any selected extra
before you confirm. Read any disclosed shared-ride/solo-fallback price before
choosing a shared option. Do not assume every service, vehicle or route has
the same price. Mandatory taxes and charges must be disclosed as applicable;
this document does not authorise an undisclosed surcharge.

Ride Now normally requires payment after completion. Scheduled rides normally
require a 10% advance, calculated and rounded by the service. Discounts or an
available booking credit may reduce the advance, including to zero. The
advance forms part of the fare; it is not an extra booking charge. Pay only
the remaining amount shown after crediting an advance or adjustment.

Use the payment options displayed for your booking, including supported UPI
or cash recording. Never give a captain or support person your UPI PIN, OTP or
bank password. Payment confirmation and receipts should be checked against
the booking. Contact support about a duplicate, missing or incorrect charge.

## Cancellations and changes

The separate Refund and Cancellation Policy gives the current product rules,
including fee-free reasons, the scheduled cancellation cap and refunds.
Review the amount displayed before confirming cancellation. A change in
pickup, destination, vehicle or time may require a new price and confirmation;
it does not give anyone permission to add undisclosed charges.

## Conduct and safety

Follow road-safety, seatbelt, lawful capacity and applicable transport rules.
Do not threaten, harass, discriminate against or abuse captains, co-riders or
staff. Do not carry prohibited goods or ask a captain to drive unlawfully.
Captains must not demand an undisclosed surcharge or pressure you to cancel.
Report unsafe conduct with the booking reference when it is safe to do so.

For an immediate emergency, contact public emergency services on 112 first.
Our booking and support channels do not replace police, ambulance or fire
services. Location updates and estimated arrival times can be delayed or
inaccurate and must not be your only safety measure.

explain the reason and give you a way to provide relevant information or ask
for review. An urgent protective restriction may precede that review. An
unverified allegation is not, by itself, proof of wrongdoing.

You may stop using the service and request account deletion. An active ride
must first be completed or cancelled through the supported process. Closure
does not cancel an existing refund, payment, complaint or legal obligation.
Record retention is explained in the Rider Privacy Notice.
