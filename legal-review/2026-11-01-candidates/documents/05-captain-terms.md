## Eligibility, vehicle and documents

Use only your own approved account and an approved vehicle appropriate for
the ride. You must be an adult and legally eligible for the vehicle and work.
Keep driving licence, registration, insurance, fitness, tax, pollution/CNG and
permit documents valid wherever required. Tell RCS immediately about expiry,
suspension, a material document change, an accident or another fact affecting
eligibility. Do not lend an account or substitute an unapproved driver/vehicle.

Submit accurate, lawfully obtained documents and use riders' information only
for the service. Approval in an app does not replace a legal vehicle or driver
requirement, and does not remove RCS's verification responsibilities.

## Offers and accepted rides

Availability and ride offers depend on demand, location, vehicle eligibility
and service rules. We do not guarantee a volume of work or earnings. These
terms do not impose exclusivity or prohibit lawful work for another service.
Handle accepted commitments safely before changing availability or stopping
work. Do not accept conflicting rides you cannot serve.

For an accepted ride, proceed to the disclosed pickup, use accurate status
updates, check the passenger/booking as appropriate and follow the lawful
route/service arrangement. Keep the app's required location permissions
enabled while online or serving a ride. If a device or network problem makes
status inaccurate, contact support when safe.

## Conduct and safety

Drive lawfully, wear seatbelts and enforce lawful capacity. Do not drive under
the influence or while unfit, harass or discriminate, threaten anyone, or
misuse a passenger's details. Do not demand an undisclosed charge, ask a rider
to cancel to avoid rules or collect the same fare twice. Do not contact a rider
after the trip for an unrelated purpose.

For an immediate emergency call 112 first, and report the incident to support
when safe. Cooperate with lawful incident and insurance procedures. The app
does not replace statutory safety duties or public emergency services. This
document does not represent that a particular training, insurance or control
room service is already supplied by RCS.

## Payments and cancellations

Follow the Captain Payments Policy and booking-specific fare/settlement
information. Use the supported cancellation flow and give a truthful reason.
Before the ride starts, the current flow supports captain cancellation while
assigned, en route or arrived. Started rides require appropriate support or
emergency handling. A cancellation may return the rider booking to matching
and may create a responsibility-review record.

A report or cancellation record is not automatically a proven disciplinary
finding. Any charge or account action must have a lawful and disclosed basis.
You may inspect relevant statements and challenge errors. This document does
not introduce an automatic fine, raw-complaint strike threshold or forfeiture
of earned money.

## Restrictions, complaints and review

RCS may apply a proportionate protective restriction for a credible safety
risk, suspected fraud, invalid documents or a legal requirement. Where
circumstances allow, it should explain the reason, identify the information
you can respond to and provide an accessible review route. A protective
restriction does not by itself prove misconduct. Sensitive information may
need to be withheld where law or another person's safety requires it.

Use the Captain Grievance Policy to dispute a deduction, allegation or
restriction and provide relevant evidence. Corrected decisions should lead
to corresponding account and financial corrections. Nothing here authorises
retaliation for a good-faith complaint or use of lawful remedies.

## Leaving and responsibility

You may request to leave and close your account. Safely resolve accepted
active rides through the supported process. A balance or dispute must be
reconciled lawfully; account closure does not forfeit undisputed earned money
or erase records that must remain. The current app may prevent deletion while
a ride is active. Privacy/retention rules are in the Captain Privacy Notice.

duties. These terms do not create unlimited indemnity, waive mandatory worker
or transport protections, or exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.
Indian law applies. You may use a competent court or statutory authority;
these terms do not impose compulsory arbitration or an exclusive court location.
