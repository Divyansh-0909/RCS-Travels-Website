# Account and service terms reference

## Draft operator and service terms

**Draft:** existing legal pages identify the operator as RAJUDDIN trading as R C S TRAVELS, a proprietorship. They cover website, WhatsApp booking, and captain-app services. Effective dates, licence position, final contract, liability, jurisdiction, and dispute clauses still require confirmation. This corpus is not evidence of an aggregator licence or legal launch clearance.

## Account accuracy and security

**Draft service expectations:** provide accurate account and booking information, use your own approved account, keep control of the registered phone, and protect authentication codes. Report suspected misuse. Captains must not lend their account or substitute an unapproved vehicle or driver.

## Rider age and booking for another person

**Draft:** the rider terms state a minimum account age of 15. Guardian consent, solo-minor travel, and bookings for other people remain unresolved. Do not treat that draft age as proof a minor can travel alone or bypass actual signup validation. Provide another person's information only with appropriate authorization.

## Availability and third-party services

A booking request is subject to availability and operational constraints. Traffic, road conditions, safety restrictions, and third-party authentication, mapping, messaging, notification, or payment failures can affect service. No universal punctuality or uninterrupted-service guarantee is established here.

## Account exit and record preservation

Riders must cancel or complete active rides before using the supported account-deletion flow. Deletion does not erase every booking, financial, dispute, or record-keeping obligation. Captains should resolve outstanding trips and financial issues through supported flows or support. Final exit and termination notice terms remain draft.

## Pending legal and publication terms

**Pending:** final terms acceptance/versioning, liability limits, statutory obligations, taxes, insurance commitments, regulatory minimum fare shares, formal appeal rules, and effective dates require owner/legal review. Do not infer compliance or waive applicable rights from a retrieved FAQ passage.
