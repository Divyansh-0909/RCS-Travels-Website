# Driver payment

Drivers ke liye abhi supported payment aur cancellation niyam.

## Yeh page kya cover karta hai

This page describes payment-related rules currently supported in the driver app. It does not create a driver marketplace deposit, a 12% fee or a 10% fee; those are not live terms for driver-partners.

## Ride ka kiraya

Drivers must use the fare and payment status shown or authorised for the booking and must not impose an undisclosed off-platform surcharge. [TO CONFIRM: fare calculation, toll/parking/waiting handling, cash/online payment methods and any permitted adjustments.]

## Scheduled rides

Pickup ke paas cancellation fee lagu hone par yeh fare ka 10%, adhiktam ₹100 hai. Listed valid reasons par fee nahi lagti. Sirf fee rakhi jaati hai aur baaki advance refund hota hai.

## Customer cancellation

The server determines a scheduled-rider cancellation quote. Drivers must not ask customers to make off-platform changes to that result.
