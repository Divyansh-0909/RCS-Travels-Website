# Grievance Redressal

How riders can raise and escalate a concern.

## Urgent safety

For an emergency or immediate safety risk, call 112 first. Then contact us with the booking details when you can.

## What you can raise

You may contact us about bookings, fares, payments, cancellations, refunds, driver conduct, safety, account access, privacy requests or other service concerns.
