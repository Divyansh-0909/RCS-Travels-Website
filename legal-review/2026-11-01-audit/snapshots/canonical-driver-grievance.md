# Driver Grievance

How driver-partners can raise a concern.

## Urgent safety

For an immediate emergency or safety risk, call 112 first. Then report the booking and incident to support when it is safe to do so.

## What you can raise

Driver-partners may raise concerns about onboarding, documents, ride allocation, fares, settlement, cancellations, fines, complaints, suspension, safety, privacy or account access.

## How to raise a concern

Contact Rajuddin with your driver account phone number, booking details, documents or screenshots that help, and the outcome you seek.
