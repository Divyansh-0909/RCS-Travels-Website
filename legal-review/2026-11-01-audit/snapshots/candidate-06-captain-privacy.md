## Information and purposes

We handle profile/contact details, authentication identifiers, profile photo,
driving licence and vehicle/eligibility documents you provide; vehicle details;
offer/ride/status and cancellation history; payment, UPI payout verification,
commission, holds, wallet, payout and financial-dispute records; complaints,
evidence, notices, responses and appeals; and device/app/notification/security
information. Only information relevant to a legitimate function should be
requested.

These records are used to verify eligibility, arrange rides, support navigation
and pickup, calculate/reconcile money, investigate complaints, notify you of
decisions, consider a response/appeal, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal
requirements. Information comes from you, your device, booked rides/riders,
providers and lawful support or regulatory interactions.

## Precise and background location

The captain app is designed to collect precise location while you are online,
including in the background, for nearby offers, dispatch and live ride
progress. Depending on the supported device/feature, observations can include
coordinates, timestamp, accuracy, speed and bearing. Location also supports
pickup evidence and operational review.

Device permission settings control access. The app may require relevant
permissions to go online. Going offline is intended to stop availability
reporting, subject to completing accepted work and device/network behaviour.
You may change permissions or close the app, but doing so can prevent matching
or interrupt ride updates. We do not claim background location works identically
on every phone or that an offline switch instantly erases prior records.

## Sharing and providers

Riders receive relevant captain identity/photo, vehicle, contact and ride
location/status information for their booking. Rider-created share links may
also make relevant captain and vehicle/trip details visible to recipients.
Authorised support/review staff receive information needed to resolve the
matter; it must not become general access to your documents or private records.

Current integrations include Clerk authentication, Google mapping/routing,
Firebase notifications and Razorpay-related payment/payout functions where
activated. WhatsApp/Meta handles messages if that channel is used. Hosting,
database and storage providers process relevant service records. Each provider's
own notice may apply to independent processing. This notice does not guarantee
that every provider processes all information only in India.

Relevant records may be disclosed for a lawful authority request, urgent
safety issue, accounting/claims advice or a business transfer with appropriate
safeguards. The request and scope must be assessed where permitted. We do not
sell personal data or authorise unrelated use of passenger information.

## Account action and your response

Dispatch and eligibility decisions can use location, documents, existing rides,
account status and service records. Complaints and financial responsibility
may involve a separate review. You may ask for relevant reasons, correct
inaccurate information and use the Captain Grievance Policy to challenge a
material adverse decision. This notice does not promise that every decision
is wholly manual or that a raw complaint is a proven finding.

## Retention, deletion and security

Document, trip, finance, location, support and security records have different
purposes. They must not all be retained indefinitely merely because they are
linked to a stable account identifier. Relevant lawful retention and dispute
holds can require some records to remain after account closure.

The current deletion route removes the live profile identifiers listed in
that flow, takes the account offline and deletes its current-location record;
profile-photo and authentication-provider cleanup can require follow-up if
the provider step fails. It preserves linked ride, wallet and audit history
and does not itself delete every uploaded eligibility document or historical
record. Request a record-specific review if you need further deletion.

We use appropriate access and security controls and require relevant providers
to protect information for their role. No system is completely secure. Report
unauthorised account/document access promptly. Legal incident-reporting duties
apply independently of an individual complaint.

## Requests and changes

Contact the person above to seek access/correction, withdraw a consent, request
closure/deletion or raise a grievance. We may verify identity proportionately.
Do not send passwords, UPI PINs or unnecessary document copies. If a request
cannot be fully met because of a legal duty or a pending claim, we should
explain the relevant exception where permitted.

We apply Indian privacy requirements according to their commencement and
scope; this notice does not treat every DPDP duty as already in force. Any
material change of purpose requires the notice/consent mandated by law. Your
statutory remedies and applicable request deadlines remain available.
