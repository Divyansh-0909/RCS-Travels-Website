# RCS owner decisions

Read the corresponding ID in [the audit](README.md) before answering. These
choices do not authorise automatic code, money-flow or publication changes.

| Decision | Choices / facts needed | What is mandatory versus optional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Rider accounts and children — D04** | A: adult account holders, children accompanied by an adult. B: support minors through a properly designed guardian/consent and travel-safety flow. State any other proposed child-travel model explicitly. | The accompanied-child wording in the candidate was my proposal. A universal 18+ passenger rule was not established. Contracting capacity and applicable child-data protections must be respected. |
| **2. Captain cancels — D05** | A: keep matching, with a clear rider choice to cancel/refund. B: end the booking and refund rather than rematch. Define when replacement attempts end. | Actual product and refund promises must match. Mandatory missed-pickup and cancellation remedies remain separate. |
| **3. Cancellation recovery — L02–L05** | Decide the operational design for ordinary captain fault, the distinct failed-pickup case, rider recovery/allocation and platform cancellation. Specify evidence/review and how credits are funded. | Apply the operative rules for each territory. The UP clauses are resolved source text, rather than optional penalty amounts. A shorter text cannot fix incompatible recovery timing/allocation. |
| **4. Captain economics — L06** | Confirm vehicle ownership, current commission policy, fare basis, pass-through costs, cash/direct-UPI handling and settlement timing. | The current nominal 10% commission is not itself a below-80% violation. Applicable minimum shares and daily settlement conditions still govern. |
| **5. Support ownership — L01, L10, F01** | Who handles incoming complaints and deadline tracking? What records demonstrate acknowledgement/redress? What arrangements serve the required operating hours for covered services? | Applicable grievance timing and safety operations cannot be treated as merely optional proposed targets. Future 2027 additions need their own readiness work. |
| **6. Refund service target — F03** | Choose an achievable initiation/follow-up target and identify the actual provider windows. | Bank-credit timing is not entirely controlled by RCS. Accepted refunds must meet applicable reasonable/statutory timing. Do not promise an unverified fixed bank-credit date. |
| **7. Driver and vehicle checks — L07–L09** | Provide evidence of police/identity/medical/experience checks, PUC renewal, training and the applicable insurance/social-security arrangements. Identify which are manual. | A required control needs real evidence. Updating privacy/terms to say a document exists is not completion of the check. |
| **8. Retention and security — L12–L13, F02** | Decide category-specific purposes, custodians, legal holds, deletion processes, log location and incident owner. Reuse applicable existing retention work only after its scope is verified. | Separate transport data limits, security-log retention and financial/legal records. One global retention number cannot safely replace those categories. |
| **9. Territory and model — J01–J08** | Provide current fleet counts/ownership by territory, actual counterparties and the precise operating model. Resolve the entity/operating prerequisites and obtain the missing current state texts. | The confirmed eight-state/UT footprint requires jurisdiction-specific assessment. A local fleet threshold or vehicle permit is not a universal platform exemption. |
| **10. One policy version — D01–D03, D06, L14** | After the substantive choices above, choose one effective version and reconcile English, Hindi, Hinglish, native links, PDF and knowledge files. Define the captain agreement acceptance/version record. | Current false/stale disclosures should be corrected; an unapproved English candidate should not silently become the Hindi contract. |
