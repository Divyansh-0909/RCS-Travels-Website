# RCS Travels — law, documents and product contradictions

Reviewed 1 November 2026, India time. Operating scope confirmed by the owner:
**Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh,
and Jammu & Kashmir.**

The installed RCS Legal Workbench was applied to **51 document units**: eight
canonical policies, 24 locale policies, eight November candidates, nine support
knowledge documents and two PDF packs. Every English route differs from the
canonical source; all 16 Hindi/Hinglish route comparisons have structural,
marker or figure drift. These counts describe differences against a newer
source revision, not proof that every translation is linguistically wrong.

**No policy, charge, account rule or production setting has been changed by
this audit.** The decisions below are for the owner. A document change does not
implement a missing operational control. Mandatory rows require a compliant
implementation or a lawful change in operating scope/model; owner-choice rows
identify optional policies.

## Verified law versus product / operational evidence

UP-specific rows apply when the described RCS activity falls within the final
UP rules. The actual service model and local fleet facts must be established;
the source is the **final 22 May 2026 notification**, not the March consultation.
The legal summaries below are intentionally brief; each citation identifies
the full rule and page for inspection.

## What is already consistent in the inspected code

- A scheduled advance is part-payment, with 10% server calculation/rounding,
  rather than an extra fee. A zero required advance can confirm without checkout.
- A qualifying scheduled rider cancellation is capped at 10%, ₹100 and the
  amount actually paid. Disclosed valid reasons waive it. See L04 for the separate
  UP recovery/allocation issue; a matching numerical cap does not settle that.
- A changed cancellation quote requires reconfirmation. The service does not
  silently charge the revised higher amount in the inspected route.
- The candidate's rejection of an unlimited indemnity is protective wording,
  despite the lexical scanner flagging those words. It was reviewed in context.
- New financial responsibility services distinguish reviewed incidents from
  mere raw complaints. Their existence does not establish production activation.
