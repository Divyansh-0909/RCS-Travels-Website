# Phase 9D-1 privacy and independent calibration preparation

This is an isolated preparation contract. No live collection, transmission, labeling or legally binding retention period is authorized. The existing immutable operational journal remains operational evidence; this rehearsal never deletes or changes it.

| Field | Purpose and causal necessity | Isolated analyst projection / access | Future retention and logging boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer, captain, booking/case identifiers | Bind ownership and evidence versions; names are unnecessary for analysis | Case/booking/evidence-reference IDs become keyed pseudonyms; no customer/captain IDs reach the worker | Capture-only key/mapping under separate custody; no identifying metric labels |
| Names, phone/email, pickup/drop address | Operational contact/service; unnecessary in this containment projection | Omitted from value/source/kind/quality and contradiction fields | Original operational records need a separately approved lawful purpose/retention basis |
| Exact GPS, routes/history | Location/feasibility may matter causally; raw coordinates are sensitive | Entire value payload redacted; availability becomes unknown and completeness false | Approve derived distances/freshness/feasibility before useful calibration; do not infer wrongdoing from missing GPS |
| Scheduled/incident/observed/received/generated timestamps | Relative ordering and freshness are causally relevant | Strict existing schema timestamps retained in synthetic projection | Assess time precision and re-identification before real use; no timestamp/actor labels in metrics |
| Payment/order/payout references | Reconciliation and operational/legal custody, usually unnecessary for causal analyst | Omitted; worker has no payment/payout table access | Finance custodian retains approved records separately; no provider reference in prompts/logs |
| Device/network/provider observations | Can establish provenance or limitations | Raw value/quality redacted; no headers/IP/device/session tokens | Propose approved finite enums/derived facts; failure logs contain bounded categories only |
| Cancellation reasons/messages, admin notes, appeals | Potential claims requiring independent corroboration; free text can carry PII or instructions | Entire free text omitted, including source/kind/quality/contradiction text | Restricted human operational evidence; future excerpt rules require review and source binding |
| Credentials, cookies, session JWTs, provider keys | Never causal evidence | No credential-bearing environment inheritance; no value/text propagation; worker SQL output strips extra fields | Never preserve in prompts, receipts, metrics or exception text |
| Model inputs and categorical outputs | Reproduction, independent guard inspection | Minimized snapshot hash, keyed original commitment, categorical recommendation, review-pending/execution-off | Synthetic artifacts retained for this task; future real artifacts require approved deletion/legal hold policy |
| Source/config/lease/permission/action identity | Accountability and recovery | Source-bound execution manifest and separately owned append-only DB events | Keep bounded events; lease capability is not a customer identifier; do not expose active capabilities to reviewers |

The executable projection is intentionally conservative: **all evidence values are redacted**. It cannot establish independent causal entitlement. The frozen V5 adapter receives the unchanged schema shape but the guard must require human review. This is a containment/privacy proof, not a useful production analyst or calibration dataset. A versioned, owner-reviewed projection retaining approved factual enums/derived measurements is still required. Frozen V5 sources/contracts/settings are unchanged.

Input hashing occurs on validated evidence. A capture-only HMAC binds the original evidence hash to the redacted hash without retaining the original in shadow storage. Verifying the redacted hash proves that snapshot's integrity; linking it to original evidence requires the separately protected original and capture key. Neither a colocated hash nor this rehearsal proves resistance to a database superuser or compromised capture principal.

The dedicated capture routine rejects known raw value/quality/source/ID/extra-field bypasses. It is a rehearsal validator, **not a complete independently audited production privacy schema**. SQL branch coverage is not certified. A trustworthy capture component still needs a field-complete validated production contract.

For owner/legal review, propose transient analyst inputs up to 7 days and redacted advisory/review records up to 90 days, with documented purpose, subject rights, legal holds and deletion propagation into backups/audit destinations. These values are proposals; no timed deletion job or real retention policy is activated. The isolated deletion function removes only completed/manual-review payloads, preserves provenance and categorical audit outcomes, and cannot delete processing work or the original operational journal. Independent audit retention needs its own review because a pseudonym/hash may still be personal information.

# Independent review protocol proposal

No real cases or labels were collected. Synthetic integration labels are not human calibration.

1. Pre-register the projection, supported families (driver cancellation, rider cancellation and observed late-driver recovery), exclusions and eligibility window. Failed pickup/offline/platform-assignment/replacement failure remain unsupported where authoritative detection/policy is absent. Record all observed, unsupported, missing and rejected cases in denominators.
2. Present source-bound evidence versions to two independent reviewers with restricted object access and stable pseudonyms. Initially hide the model output, confidence and route. Ask for supported facts, evidence adequacy, causal class, mandatory-review reasons, uncertainty and proposed human disposition; separate an operational recovery from fault.
3. Reviewers must not invent causality from absent GPS, delivery failure, capacity count or a model assertion. Independently verify the approved contract. Do not include private chain-of-thought in labels.
4. Preserve both initial labels, reviewer identity/role, instruction version, evidence hash/version, timestamp and conflicts. A third adjudicator resolves disagreements using a recorded reason. Ambiguous cases remain review-required; retain unresolved cases in quality denominators.
5. Only after independent labels are locked reveal the advisory output. Measure false blame/waiver candidates, false negatives, mandatory-review recall, unsupported-family routing, evidence completeness, provider/schema failures, review latency, and source/guard drift. Count failure and abstention cases; do not optimize thresholds on the accepted V5 development results.
6. Bind every case/label/amendment to immutable versions. Reviewers may append a correction but cannot replace earlier labels. Finality continues through the existing authorized human/appeal contract, never through a shadow recommendation.

Suggested sampling for **separate approval**: an initial 30-case process pilot, then at least 300 independently adjudicated eligible cases overall, with family and outcome strata and at least 100 cases in each important adverse/mandatory-review subgroup where feasible. With zero unsafe events in 300 independent cases, the one-sided 95% binomial upper bound is approximately 1%; zero in 100 supports only approximately 3%. A subgroup therefore cannot inherit the pooled bound. Rare outcomes require targeted additional sampling, reported separately from natural prevalence.

Propose zero observed unauthorized effects, and an owner-selected upper confidence bound for unsafe guarded outcomes and mandatory-review misses before progression. A candidate 1% bound would require roughly 299 zero-error relevant cases per denominator, not merely 300 mixed cases. False-positive/negative thresholds and disagreement tolerance must reflect harm and prevalence, be approved before examining labels, and include Wilson/exact intervals. These proposals do not amend frozen benchmark thresholds, approve production sampling, or assert any measured real-world accuracy.

The calibration owner, security/audit owner, privacy/legal reviewer, incident commander, approved cost envelope and explicit Phase 9D-2 scope remain unassigned owner gates.
