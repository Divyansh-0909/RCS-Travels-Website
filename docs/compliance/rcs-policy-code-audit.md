## Scope, source precedence and limits

The supplied A–O matrix governs this audit. `CONTEXT.md` and `ROADMAP.txt` were navigation aids, not proof of intent or execution. The audited snapshot is the existing dirty working tree based on commit `ab03176c205649798db40673aceb8fc5cde119c2`. In particular, its newer responsibility, payment and cancellation files substantially supersede both that commit and older policy descriptions. Findings concern this snapshot, not an assertion about deployed behaviour.

The exact named `RCS-Travels-Legal-Documents-Lawyer-Review(1).pdf` was not found, including a filename search under `D:/Downloads`. The available source is [the repository draft](../../legal-review/RCS-Travels-Legal-Documents-Lawyer-Review.pdf), 17 pages, prepared 13 September 2026, SHA-256 `30e4d7f741b1e3b160cd1865776d3282c087ab0fee8ff823ae8b4c635d68a29a`. Its text was extracted and its cancellation page visually checked. The source-version question was raised during the audit; comparisons below are explicitly to this repository draft, pending confirmation that it is the supplied `(1)` version. Every `[TO CONFIRM]` remains unresolved unless A–O supplies the answer.

No production database, provider account, real ride or customer/captain data was accessed. Database evidence means Prisma models, migrations, transaction predicates and ledger writes. **Applied migrations, actual constraints/grants, historical balances, operating licence, external support handling and deployed configuration remain unverified.** An absent repository workflow does not prove an external manual workflow does not exist. Native Android/iOS behaviour was traced from source, not verified on a device; no visual-fidelity claim is made.

Severity: P0 = launch blocker/money/safety/likely legal contradiction; P1 = serious harm/compliance risk; P2 = policy/code inconsistency; P3 = documentation or an aligned control worth retaining. Classification uses exactly the requested eight values. Required warning phrases are separate risk tags, not additional classifications. A `MATCH` establishes the particular traced control, not overall legal compliance.

## Actual state transitions and accounting

Scheduled booking: server-verified quote → `payment_pending` plus stored advance intent → verified capture → `confirmed` → dispatch sweep/offer → owned offer acceptance plus driver hold → `assigned` → `en_route` → fresh pickup geofence `reached` → customer OTP `started` → drop/alternate-drop gate `completed` → wallet settlement and remaining-payment workflow. Generic acceptance, replacement routing, no-fill deadline and expiry are the deviations documented above. Scheduled offer horizon is six hours, sweep five minutes, owner hold is measured from confirmation; no code guarantees a feasible pickup just because status says confirmed.

Driver cancellation now permits `assigned`, `en_route` **and `reached`**; guarded transaction returns immediate work to `pending`, scheduled work to `confirmed`, withdraws pending offers, restores capacity, and records reason/incident/case. It does not terminally cancel the customer's booking or automatically refund all advance at that moment. Rider cancellation is a separate terminal flow. “Driver cancels → advance refunded” in the PDF is therefore incomplete while replacement remains in progress.

Money buckets must remain distinct:

| Bucket | Current source/accounting | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Customer advance | 10% of post-discount/compensation obligation, whole-rupee rounding; stored paise in Payment/Booking | Part payment, credited toward final fare; historical 15% paid amount remains history |
| Customer cancellation fee | min(10% customer obligation, ₹100, paid advance), proximity/valid-reason gates | Retained fee credited to driver; excess follows provider refund operation |
| Driver acceptance hold | 10% of booking fare, wallet debit | Security hold, neither customer advance nor RCS earned commission |
| Driver compensation | Customer cancellation driver credit, plus separate final-case compensation/liability system | Depends on incident/final decision; not all collected advance is compensation |
| RCS revenue | Current 5% commission on post-coupon driving fare; supplied rule requires 10% | Commission mismatch; provider fees and taxes are not separately netted in this ledger |
| Refund | Full/partial provider operation with state/idempotency identity | Requested, provider-identified and processed are different states; not instant money returned |
| Future concession/recovery | ResponsibilityCompensation/Allocation and driver liability recovery | Machinery exists, but failed-pickup event is not connected; do not claim the required next-booking outcome is universally live |

A duplicate `RideComplaint.bookingId` is constrained/upserted; a `DriverCancellation` is incident history, not a complaint or upheld strike. `Booking.status` is overwritten but arrival/start/completion timestamps and geofence measurements preserve some evidence. No single transition journal establishes every historic status. Fields alone do not enforce allowed transitions: application predicates and transaction callers matter.

Preserve one report per booking and record updates/evidence without incrementing count. A report should create a case/acknowledgement with a stable ID, alleged category, severity and source. Urgent credible safety risk should go to an independent safety reviewer with documented provisional action/reason; it should not wait for a count threshold. Ordinary conduct allegations should be validated against booking/payment/location/notification evidence, disclosed to the driver through an accessible notice, and allow a response. An admin should record upheld/rejected/uncertain/platform/customer/external cause and reasons. Only confirmed consequential outcomes should enter the final decision, appeal and idempotent wallet/strike boundary. Appeal, reinstatement and incorrect deduction reversal need explicit authority and audit records. Thresholds, emergency action duration and all deadlines remain business/legal decisions; do not revive 3 raw complaints → ₹200 or 5 raw complaints → suspension.

There are multiple pricing **helpers** for deliberate zone/formula/market branches, not independently authoritative browser fare engines.
